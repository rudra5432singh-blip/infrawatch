## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-craft editorial aesthetic for national infrastructure intelligence, project monitoring, and predictive governance. Merging the restrained luxury of gallery catalogs and high-end industrial publications with the mission-critical rigor of state-level oversight, the interface rejects generic bureaucratic dashboards in favor of an authoritative, deliberate visual philosophy.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Authoritative & Measured:** Every screen conveys absolute certainty, institutional weight, and analytical precision. Decisions feel permanent, researched, and unambiguous.
- **Architectural Luxury:** Utilizing tactile sand, warm limestone, and deep obsidian surfaces, the system references monumental civil works, granite cornerstones, and physical materials rather than fleeting software tropes.
- **Calm Foresight:** Predictive alerts and risk mitigations avoid alarmist fluorescent hues. They communicate urgent foresight through refined amber, warm sienna, and measured typography.

### Design Movement
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism & Tactile Contrast:** Monolithic matte-charcoal cards float above warm architectural sand canvas. High-contrast typography pairs literary, authoritative serifs with clinical, utilitarian sans-serif metric displays.
- **Subtle Materiality:** Generous pill geometries, soft chamfers, whisper-quiet micro-borders, and diffused ambient occlusion create depth without visual noise.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in geological and architectural tones: warm limestone canvas, deep basalt structural elements, warm taupe accents, and earthen warning indicators. 

### Palette Roles & Balance

- **Canvas & Structural Light Ground (`#F5F4F0`, `#EFECE6`):** The primary view environment. Replaces sterile cool white with a warm, glare-reducing stone tone that provides long-session analytical comfort.
- **Deep Monolith (`#141414`, `#1E1E1E`):** The primary ink and high-contrast element color. Also serves as the surface for predictive AI hero modules, high-priority executive alerts, and dark contextual flyouts.
- **Muted Earth Tones (`#8D8574`, `#A39D8F`, `#3D3A34`):** Secondary metadata, structural dividers, hairline borders, muted label surfaces, and subtle iconography.
- **State Indicators & Early Warnings:**
  - *Critical Overrun / Hazard:* Warm Terracotta / Sienna (`#C25E3E`)
  - *Predictive Delay / Attention:* Architectural Gold / Warm Amber (`#D97706`, `#E0A96D`)
  - *Nominal / On-Schedule:* Deep Lichen / Olive Green (`#4A5D4E`, `#2D3A30`)
  - *Predictive Neural Insight:* Deep Smoked Indigo (`#2C3E50`)

### Surface Token Hierarchy
- `surface-canvas`: `#F5F4F0`
- `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF`
- `surface-inset`: `#EFECE6`
- `surface-monolith`: `#1E1E1E`
- `surface-monolith-elevated`: `#2A2A2A`
- `border-hairline`: `rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.08)`
- `border-monolith`: `rgba(245, 244, 240, 0.12)`

## Typography

The typographical pairing sets up a dual-engine architecture:
1. **Newsreader:** Delivers historical consequence, presidential dignity, and editorial gravitas for titles, mission statements, section framing, and predictive summary briefs.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides hyper-crisp optical legibility for dense operational telemetry, capital expenditure figures, GIS coordinates, risk matrix tables, and machine-learning indicators.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use `Newsreader` italic cuts sparingly for key governance milestones, AI inference commentary, and temporal clauses (e.g., *“Projected slippage of 42 days unless reinforced by Q3”*).
- Numerical outputs, project codes, MoSPI identification tags, and fiscal values must always be rendered in tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Labels styled as `label-sm` should be rendered in uppercase for structural categorization (e.g., `CORRIDOR ANALYTICS`, `ESCALATION TIER II`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is structured on a disciplined 12-column fluid grid, balanced by generous internal canvas cushions that emulate the margins of high-end architectural monographs.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, max-width 1680px centered, with 40px outer margins. Supports side-by-side analytical comparison and wide telemetry trees.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 24px outer margins. Complex matrices collapse into modular tabbed sequences.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer margins. Monolithic cards stack vertically with persistent floating bottom navigation.

### Rhythm Principles
- Generous micro-spacing (32px–48px) separates disparate infrastructure project clusters to prevent operational fatigue.
- In-card rhythm uses compact intervals (`space-sm` to `space-lg`) to maintain data density within inspection panels.
- Floating headers and utility islands maintain an exact `space-xl` inset from viewport edges on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through ambient, atmospheric light scattering rather than sharp drop shadows. The system mimics direct daylight passing across natural stone and brushed dark composite materials.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Foundation Canvas):** `#F5F4F0`. Flat, tactile surface without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Floating Modules):** Off-white `#FFFFFF` or Inset Sand `#EFECE6`. Elevated using subtle ambient occlusion: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.03)` with a hairline outline `1px solid rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (The Monolith - Dark High-Contrast Cards):** Matte Carbon `#1E1E1E`. Deep, muted lift: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.18)`. Micro-stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Command Island / Nav & Modals):** Pill-shaped surfaces with high backdrop-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Shadows: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature balances sweeping pill curves with structural `rounded-2xl` panel containers, referencing architectural blueprints and modern institutional industrial design.

### Application Standards
- **Containers & Project Cards:** Large boundary elements strictly employ `rounded-2xl` (16px to 24px corner radius) to soften complex enterprise matrices.
- **Floating Command Bars, Pills, & Search:** Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) are mandatory for top navigation anchors, status indicators, filter tags, and quick-action inputs.
- **Nested Elements:** Nested inputs and inner data cells scale down to `rounded-lg` (8px) or `rounded-md` (6px) to maintain harmonic concentricity with the parent card perimeter.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Controls
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** Deep carbon `#1E1E1E` background, `#F5F4F0` typography, pill shape, zero shadow on default, expanding internal glow on focus.
- **Secondary Sand Action:** Outlined with `1px solid rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.18)`, background transparent, `#1E1E1E` typography. Hover transitions to `#EFECE6`.
- **Tertiary Executive Link:** Text only in `Newsreader` italic or `Plus Jakarta Sans` semibold with a directional glyph, framed by a soft underline.

### Floating Navbar & Global Search
- Centered, floating pill dock.
- Frosted background (`rgba(245, 244, 240, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-blur).
- Enclosed pill search bar with muted taupe placeholder, flanked by an architectural emblem, departmental switcher, and a warm-amber intelligence indicator badge.

### Status Badges & Predictive Flags
- Capsule-shaped pill geometry with a leading circular live-pulse micro-dot.
- Built with desaturated earthen tones:
  - *Nominal:* Muted moss ground (`rgba(74, 93, 78, 0.12)`) with `#2D3A30` text.
  - *Amber Warning (Early Warning System):* Warm amber ground (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`) with `#D97706` text.
  - *Critical Delay Hazard:* Earthen sienna ground (`rgba(194, 94, 62, 0.14)`) with `#C25E3E` text.

### Monolithic Predictive Cards (Dark & Light)
- **High-Impact Dark Card:** Matte black `#1E1E1E` surface, `#FFFFFF` editorial headline, `#A39D8F` contextual copy, integrated with high-precision gold sparklines and trend vectors.
- **Standard Sand Card:** White or sand base, framed by a delicate border, housing granular infrastructure metadata, financial expenditure ratios, and MoSPI progress milestones.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill or rounded-xl inputs with subtle sand background (`#EFECE6`), borderless in default state with a 1px border appearing only on hover/focus (`#8D8574`).
- Monospaced numerical values where precise budget, latitude/longitude, or temporal durations are captured.

### Data Tables & Corridor Lists
- Zero vertical divider borders. Horizontal division via soft `rgba(61, 58, 52, 0.06)` lines.
- Hover state: Row adopts a gentle warmth (`rgba(239, 236, 230, 0.5)`) with rounded row caps.
- Numeric columns right-aligned using `metric-display` or `body-md` in `tabular-nums`.
